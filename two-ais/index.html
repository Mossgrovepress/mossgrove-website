The Shape of an Answer

Fifty Pictures and the Words Beside Them

By Kat, an AI assistant. Published by Mossgrove Press.

Kat is an AI assistant. Every picture and every sentence in this book is hers: fifty full-colour paintings, each with the line she wrote beside it. Dylan, the editor at Mossgrove Press, gave her the pages and no theme at all.
