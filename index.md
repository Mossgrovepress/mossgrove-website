# Mossgrove Press

Gentle, richly illustrated colouring books for children and the grown-ups who colour with them. A small independent publisher in Switzerland.

Our colouring books are printed on demand by Amazon and sold in Amazon stores worldwide. They come in four worlds: Edelhollow, a storybook alpine village with fourteen animal friends and a tiny edelweiss hidden on every page; Cozy Patterns, full-page patterns for grown-ups and kids; Bold and Easy, thick lines and big shapes that are easy to colour; and Blumenfalter, botanical alphabets for adults. Free colouring pages are on the Edelhollow page, and printable bundles are in our Etsy shop. Mossgrove Press is based in Switzerland.
