Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

Mossgrove Press ("we," "us") publishes the Edelhollow book series. This page explains what information we collect through this website and what we do with it, in plain language.

What we collect

If you use the "Free Edelhollow Download" signup form, we collect the first name and email address you enter. We don't collect anything else through this site, no tracking cookies, no analytics scripts, no third-party ad pixels.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the free coloring page you requested.
  • If you opt in, to occasionally send Edelhollow news: new books, free printables, and similar updates. We don't send anything else, and we don't sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

How it's handled

Your name and email are stored with Zoho Campaigns, an email service provider based in the EU, which we use to send these emails. We use double opt-in, meaning you'll receive one confirmation email and nothing further until you click to confirm.

Your rights

  • Every email we send includes a working unsubscribe link. One click removes you from the list.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing hello@mossgrovepress.com.

Where this applies

Mossgrove Press operates from Switzerland. We handle personal data in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and, for visitors in the EU/EEA, in line with the GDPR.

Questions

Reach us anytime at hello@mossgrovepress.com.
