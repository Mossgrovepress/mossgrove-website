Our Worlds

Edelhollow

A little village high in the Swiss Alps where the neighbors are animals and nothing much happens, in the best possible way. Home of the coloring book series that began with Cozy Mornings in Edelhollow and continues with Market Day in Edelhollow, with a tiny edelweiss hiding on every page.

Visit Edelhollow

Cozy Patterns

Full-page patterns to color: mushrooms, honey, quilts, and cats on the rooftops, for grown-ups and kids coloring the same book differently every time. Starts with Cozy Cottagecore, out now on Amazon.

Visit Cozy Patterns

Bold and Easy

Big, simple pictures to color, with thick lines, large shapes and friendly animal characters. Easy for small hands and relaxing for grown ups. Starts with Bold and Easy Halloween, out now on Amazon.

Visit Bold and Easy

Blumenfalter

Intricate botanical coloring books for grown-ups, drawn in fine engraving detail. Every subject comes as a pair, a soft Garden book and its dark Gothic twin. Starts with Garden Alphabet and Gothic Alphabet, both out now on Amazon.

Visit Blumenfalter

About

Mossgrove Press is a small independent publishing studio. We make gentle, richly illustrated books for children and the grown-ups who read with them.

Our books favor warm details, unhurried stories, and pages that reward a second look.
