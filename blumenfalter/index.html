Blumenfalter

Intricate botanical colouring books for grown-ups, in fine engraving detail.

Every Blumenfalter subject comes as a pair: a Garden book, soft and flowery, and its Gothic twin, dark and thorny, alive with moths and beetles. Both are drawn in the style of old natural-history engravings, for long, slow evenings with pencils or fine markers.

About the line

Blumenfalter is a made-up word, flower and moth in one, and it is ours alone. The line began with the Wilderkind prints, creatures woven into letters, and grew into books. Flowers and insects first, animals later, always in pairs. Made in Switzerland, like every Mossgrove Press book.
